About Chatibb

Welcome to Chatibb

Chatibb is a premium anonymous communication platform dedicated to creating direct, human-to-human conversations without barriers. We believe in organic connection, devoid of bot intervention or AI simulation. Every single user on Chatibb is a real person seeking meaningful or fun conversations.

Core Principles

100% Real Users: We use sophisticated, non-intrusive heuristic detection layers to keep bots, automation scripts, and fake users out of our ecosystem.

Privacy First: Your privacy is absolute. We do not store chat histories, IP records, or tracking data on our servers. Your conversations are ephemeral, existing only while both users are active.

Zero Registration: Skip typing emails, verifications, or passwords. Your identity remains safe under a random user tag.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Transmission

Conversations on Chatibb are routed securely through transient memory. We do not write your text communications to any disk or permanent storage systems. Once your session ends, the chat data vanishes immediately from memory.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not request names, emails, age, or phone numbers.
  • We do not log user IP addresses in correlation with chats.
  • We do not use tracking pixels or sell profile datasets.

3. Device Metadata

We process ephemeral browser details (such as language preferences and layout size) strictly to properly route matching algorithms. This data is never logged or indexed.
